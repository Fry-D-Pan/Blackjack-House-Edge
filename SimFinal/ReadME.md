# Blackjack Game / Simulation

## Basic Game
Navagaite to the BasicGame folder and run ```python3 game.py```. This will open up two windows. You first must enter the balance you want to play with. <br>
You can then place your bet on your bet and it will deal the cards. Depending what you want to do you can click any option that applies to your hand and see if you win. You must enter whole numbers as the currency.
